01 About

I'm drawn to problems where learning from data beats hand-written rules. Over the years that's meant teaching networks to translate images between domains, training agents to play games from raw pixels, and — most recently — probing the internal behavior of language models.

I grew up in Colombia and studied computing at the Universidad de los Andes, where I first got hooked on machine learning through astronomy research. Since then I've worked across Google, including time on YouTube, and now build from Paris.

My open-source work has been used and starred by thousands of people learning the same things I was once figuring out. I care about making hard ideas legible, whether that's a clean implementation, a clear diagram, or a well-written explainer.
