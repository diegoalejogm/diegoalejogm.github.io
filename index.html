03 Current research

What I'm digging into right now.

Persona vectors for implicit elicitation

Early results

Building on Anthropic's safety-research persona‑vectors work, I'm investigating whether persona vectors can monitor implicitly eliciting prompts: cases where a trait such as sycophancy is induced indirectly through roleplay or framing rather than explicit instructions. The question I'm probing is how robust activation-level monitoring stays when the elicitation is implicit.

Early results (Qwen2.5-7B): explicit-extracted vectors largely detect implicitly-elicited traits, with "evil" a revealing exception. Read the case study for details.
